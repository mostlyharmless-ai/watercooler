# Trademark policy

This policy covers the `Watercooler` and `Watercooler Cloud` names, logos,
word marks, and brand assets.

Watercooler source code may be licensed separately under Apache 2.0. That
license grants copyright rights in the code. It does not grant trademark
rights in the Watercooler brand.

## You may

- Use the `Watercooler` name to refer to the unmodified upstream project.
- State factually that your tool, plugin, or service works with Watercooler.
- Link to the upstream project, documentation, and company site.
- Use the project name in nominative fair use contexts such as:
  - "compatible with Watercooler"
  - "imports Watercooler threads"
  - "migration tool for Watercooler"

## You may not

- Use `Watercooler` or `Watercooler Cloud` in a way that suggests your fork,
  product, or service is the official project when it is not.
- Use Watercooler logos, icons, or visual brand assets in your product,
  service, app UI, or marketing without written permission.
- Ship a modified fork under the `Watercooler` name without clear written
  authorization.
- Register domains, social handles, package names, or company names that are
  confusingly similar to the Watercooler marks.

## Forks and modified versions

If you distribute a modified version of Watercooler, you may do so under the
applicable code license, but you must:

- Remove Watercooler logos and visual brand assets from the modified version.
- Rename the fork so users can tell it is not the upstream project.
- Avoid statements or UI copy that imply endorsement, certification, or an
  official relationship unless we gave written approval.

Examples:

- Allowed: `Acme Thread Server, compatible with Watercooler`
- Not allowed: `Acme Watercooler Enterprise`
- Not allowed: `Official Watercooler for Finance`

## Screenshots and editorial use

You may use screenshots of the upstream project for commentary, tutorials,
reviews, or educational material, so long as the usage does not imply
partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement.

## Commercial services

You may truthfully describe services that help customers deploy, integrate, or
operate Watercooler. You may not brand a third-party hosted offering as
`Watercooler Cloud` or otherwise present it as the official hosted service.

## Reserving rights

We reserve all trademark rights in the Watercooler marks and may update this
policy over time. Any permissions outside this policy require written approval
from Mostly Harmless AI.
